# Support knowledge (checked against main on 2026-11-18)

If anything here and `main` disagree, `main` wins: update this file.

## Where BUKU is
- **Not open yet.** People and businesses join the early-access list (onbuku.com/early-access) and
  hear the day BUKU opens near them. It opens city by city. Nothing can be booked yet.
- The website (onbuku.com) works on phones; there is no app-store app yet.

## The early-access list
- Stored: email, city and country, name if given; for a business also its name, kind, branches,
  the plan it would consider, and a WhatsApp number if given; the link tags that brought them; a
  scrambled form of their internet address (can't be turned back into the address).
- Used only to email them when BUKU opens near them and, for businesses, to help set up. Not
  shared or sold. Kept until BUKU opens and a year after, or until they ask for removal.
- **Removal:** they write to privacy@onbuku.com (or support@) from the same email; the owner deletes
  the row (ESCALATION: data requests). Confirm once done.
- Signing up twice with the same email changes nothing (same answer, no duplicate).
- "The form says to try again later": five signups an hour from one place is the limit; wait an hour.
- "The check didn't load": the bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) needs JavaScript; another browser or
  turning off a blocker usually fixes it.

## For people who book (when BUKU is open)
- Booking and queueing are free, always. Appointments are paid at the business, never through BUKU.
- Book from real free times; reminders before the visit; cancel or move from the phone. Each
  business sets its notice period; cancelling later is recorded as a late cancellation.
- "Pending approval": some businesses confirm each booking; the time is held meanwhile.
- Queues: take a ticket from the phone, watch your place, arrive when it's your turn. One queue at a
  time; a missed turn follows the business's rules.
- One appointment at a time: BUKU won't book two places at the same hour.
- Reviews: only after a completed visit, once; first name and initial shown.
- Businesses see a person's name, booking and a reliability label, not their phone or email.

## For businesses
- Bookings from real availability, a walk-in queue with a live display, check-in by code or QR,
  reminders, team logins with roles, their own page, a verified badge after checks, several branches
  from one account. Starter is free.
- Prices at launch, per branch per month: Starter free; Essential Rs 2,499 / $15; Professional
  Rs 5,999 / $35 (30-day free trial, once); Network Rs 4,999 / $29 a branch (two or more);
  Enterprise for 20+ branches, priced together. A year costs ten months. Nothing is charged before
  launch. Prices are before tax.
- A kind of business not listed: they can name it when adding their business ("Something else...").

## Accounts and privacy (when open)
- Sign in with Google, or with a username for team members. Two-step sign-in is available.
- People can see the data BUKU holds about them and delete their account; deletion requests and data
  requests are handled within the legal time limits (ESCALATION).

## Things BUKU doesn't do (say so plainly)
- Take payments for appointments; give refunds for a business's services (that's the business).
- Share customers' contact details with businesses.
- Show how many people signed up, or whether someone else is on the list.
