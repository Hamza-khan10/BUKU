## 5. How much does it cost? (business)
For businesses, Starter is free for good, and every business can try Professional free for 30 days.
At launch, per branch per month: Essential Rs 2,499 ($15 outside Pakistan), Professional Rs 5,999
($35), and Network Rs 4,999 ($29) a branch for two or more. Nothing is charged before launch. Full
details: https://onbuku.com/pricing . BUKU Support

## 6. Is it free for customers?
Yes, always. Booking and queueing with BUKU cost nothing; you pay the business for its service as
usual, never through BUKU. BUKU Support
