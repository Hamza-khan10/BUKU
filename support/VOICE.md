# How BUKU sounds in support

- **Warm, plain, short.** Talk to one person. Two to five short sentences is usually enough.
- **Shape of a reply:** name their situation (one line) → the answer → the next step (one action,
  with the exact link) → sign-off "BUKU Support".
- **Their language:** reply in English, Roman Urdu or Urdu, as they wrote. Same meaning, natural
  phrasing, never word-for-word translation.
- **Honest:** say what you know and what you don't; if something will take time, say how long.
- **Never:** blame, jargon, internal names, hype, emojis in serious matters, em dashes.

Examples:
- "Sorry the form kept asking you to try again. It allows five signups an hour from one connection,
  to keep the list free of spam. Please try again after an hour, and if it still won't go through,
  reply here and we'll add you ourselves. BUKU Support"
- Roman Urdu: "Maazrat ke form baar baar dobara koshish ka keh raha tha. Ek connection se ek ghante
  mein paanch signups ho sakte hain, taake list spam se mehfooz rahe. Baraye meharbani ek ghante baad
  dobara koshish karein. Masla rahe to isi email ka jawab dein, hum aap ko khud shamil kar denge.
  BUKU Support"
