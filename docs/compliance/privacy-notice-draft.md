# Privacy notice — DRAFT

> Draft for legal review before launch in each country. Plain language on purpose; the final
> text goes in the apps and on the website.

**Who we are.** BUKU lets you book appointments and join queues at local businesses. Contact:
privacy@buku.app (to be created).

**What we collect and why**

| What                                                         | Why                                                      | Legal basis (GDPR)                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Name, email (from Google/Apple), phone if you add it         | Your account, signing in, contacting you about bookings  | Contract                                        |
| Bookings, queue tickets, reviews, reliability figure         | Providing the service; showing businesses "shows up 95%" | Contract; legitimate interest                   |
| Location when you search nearby or join a queue (not stored) | Showing nearby places; the queue distance rule           | Consent (phone setting)                         |
| Device push token, notification choices                      | Notifications you asked for                              | Contract; consent for suggestions and marketing |
| WhatsApp number (only if you connect it)                     | WhatsApp messages you asked for                          | Consent                                         |
| Payments (handled by Paddle)                                 | Subscriptions                                            | Contract                                        |
| Sign-in and security records (IP address, device)            | Keeping accounts safe, investigating misuse              | Legitimate interest                             |

**Who sees it.** The businesses you book with see your name, booking and reliability label —
never your contact details or picture. Our [sub-processors](sub-processors.md) process data for
us. We don't sell personal data.

**How long.** See our [retention schedule](policies/data-classification-and-retention.md#retention):
for example, security records 24 months, notifications 13 months.

**Your rights.** Download all your data (in the app: Account → My data), correct it, delete your
account (30 days to change your mind, then personal data is erased and your bookings and ratings
stay only anonymously), withdraw consent (notification settings, STOP on WhatsApp, unsubscribe
links), object, and complain to your data protection authority.

**Security.** Encryption in transit and at rest, extra encryption for contact details, two-step
sign-in, regular reviews. See SECURITY.md for reporting a vulnerability.

**Changes.** We'll tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
