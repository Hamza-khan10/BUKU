# BUKU brand kit

## Name

- **BUKU**, always in capitals. The website is **onbuku.com**; the handle everywhere is **@onbuku**.
- Say "BUKU" in sentences; "onbuku" only for the address and the handle.
- Never "Buku", "Booku" or "buku app" in text. The logo's wordmark is lowercase "buku"; in sentences
  it's always **BUKU**. (Other companies use similar names: be unmistakable.)

## Promise

**No one should wait to be seen.** (Brand Identity v1.0, D-143.) It speaks to both audiences: the
person in the waiting room, and the owner whose reputation it is. For owners alone (the business
page, B2B posts): **Full books. Empty waiting rooms.** Brand idea: _Your time, held._
BUKU takes bookings and runs walk-in queues for barbers, clinics, salons, offices and 116 kinds of
place. People book or join a queue from their phone, free.

Ratio guide: Porcelain or Midnight with Ink about 60%, Emerald 30%, Saffron 10% (the dot).
Contrast: text always at least 4.5:1 (white on Emerald, Ink on Porcelain or Mint soft, Mint on Midnight).

## Type

- **Instrument Sans** for everything (Google Fonts, free): headlines semibold (600) with tight
  tracking (-0.04em at display sizes), body regular with comfortable line height (1.5).
- JetBrains Mono only for booking codes and ticket numbers.
- Headlines short: one idea, two lines at most; the second line may be Mint on Midnight.

## Logo (Brand Identity v1.0)

"U is for you": a thick U holding a Saffron dot, your place held. The wordmark is lowercase "buku" in
the same round strokes, and its last u holds the same dot. Files: `brand/logos/` (the owner's kit;
the full guidelines are in the owner's design canvas).

- `buku-logo-horizontal.svg` (default; on light), `-reversed` (on dark), `buku-logo-stacked*.svg`
  (square and tall spaces), `buku-wordmark.svg`, `buku-symbol.svg`, `buku-symbol-reversed.svg`,
  `buku-logo-mono-black.svg` / `-white.svg` (one colour), `buku-app-icon.svg` (profile pictures).
- Clear space: the dot's diameter on every side. Minimum: the lockup 120 px / 30 mm wide, the
  symbol 20 px / 6 mm; below that, the app icon.
- Never stretch, recolour, tilt, shadow, add gradients or move the dot.
- **The held dot:** the logo's dot as a full stop, once per layout, on the line that matters
  ("No one should wait to be seen●"). The ticket edge, the queue line and the board are the
  supporting patterns; the old ticket-and-check mark is retired.

## Imagery

- Real moments, not stock poses: a waiting room, a phone in hand at a bus stop, a barber between
  customers, a queue number on a screen. Warm, natural light.
- People only with written consent. Shopfronts and screens only with the owner's permission.
- Product shots: the real BUKU screens (from the site), on a phone, never mock data that looks real.
- Shapes: soft, consistent corners (8 to 20 px on 1080 px artwork); no gradients except the subtle
  Emerald glow used on the share card.

## Voice

- **Plain, warm, honest.** Short sentences. Speak to one person. Say what happens, not how amazing.
- **Concrete:** times, places, steps ("arrive when it's your turn"), not adjectives ("seamless").
- **Respectful of time:** get to the point; no clickbait.
- **Never:** hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, elevate, unleash), fake urgency, invented
  numbers, sarcasm about competitors, em dashes.
- **Languages:** English, Roman Urdu (as people text), Urdu script for wider reach. Keep the same
  meaning across all three; don't translate word for word.

Examples:

- Yes: "Your barber is busy. Take your place in line from home, and walk in when it's your turn."
- No: "Revolutionize your grooming experience with BUKU's seamless queue technology!"
