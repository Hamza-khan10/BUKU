# What BUKU is and does (claim only this)

Source of truth: `main` (the live site, onbuku.com). Last checked 2026-11-18. If something here and
`main` disagree, `main` wins: update this file.

## Status

- **BUKU hasn't opened yet.** People and businesses join the **early-access list** at
  onbuku.com/early-access and hear the day it opens near them. BUKU is **opening city by city**.
- Never say or imply that bookings work today, that businesses are listed, or name any city as
  "live".

## For people who book (free, always)

- Book a time at a place, from real free times only; get a reminder before the visit; cancel or move
  it from the phone (each place sets its notice period).
- Join a walk-in queue from the phone, see your place move, and arrive when it's your turn.
- One appointment at a time: BUKU won't book you into two places at the same hour.
- Reviews only from real, completed visits.
- Appointments are paid at the business, never through BUKU. BUKU never charges customers.

## For businesses

- Bookings from their real availability: opening hours, closures, each person's hours and time off.
  Confirm automatically, or approve each booking.
- A walk-in queue people join from nearby, with a live display; call the next ticket, add walk-ins,
  a priority lane, pause or close the queue.
- Check-in by code or QR; the day's list stays right.
- Reminders sent for them: the day before and a couple of hours before, never at night where they are.
- Each customer comes with a simple reliability label; bookings from people who often don't turn up
  can wait for approval. Late cancellations and no-shows, in numbers.
- Team logins with roles (manager, front desk, staff); removing someone ends their access at once.
- Their own page: services and prices, team, hours, photos, directions, live queue, at one link.
- Verified badge once BUKU has checked their registration details.
- Several branches: each branch is its own business on BUKU, switched between from one account.
